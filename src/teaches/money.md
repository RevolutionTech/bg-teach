# Money!

### Overview
- [ ] Theme & vibe: swap currencies, corner markets
- [ ] Objective: most points
- [ ] Game end: last deck card dealt → 1 more round → final scoring

### Round
- [ ] Bid → player turns → round end

### Bids
- [ ] Secretly choose 1+ cards from hand, facedown; reveal together
  - [ ] Value = sum of cards (gold/silver count too)
- [ ] Fake money (0 value): hide bid size; return to hand when revealed
- [ ] Ties for highest: lowest serial number on bid cards goes first

### Turns
- [ ] Highest bid goes first; must do one:
  - [ ] Swap whole bid with one side of market
  - [ ] Swap whole bid with another player's bid
    - [ ] They now hold your cards as their bid; they have highest bid → next turn
  - [ ] Keep
- [ ] After swap/keep, take bid cards into hand
- [ ] Next turn: highest bid left on table

### Round end
- [ ] Refill each market side to 4 (side I first if short)
  - [ ] Sides with 4+ don't get more

### Scoring
- [ ] Currency total in hand: ≤100 = 0; 110-190 = total − 100; 200+ = total
- [ ] Gold & silver: face value total
- [ ] Sets of 3 matching cards = +100 each (20s or 30s of one currency, gold, silver)
  - [ ] Bonus works even with weak position
  - [ ] Bonuses don't count for bids or position
