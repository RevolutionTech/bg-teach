# Bang! The Dice Game

### Overview

- [ ] Theme & vibe
- [ ] Hidden roles; Wild West shootout
- [ ] Sheriff: eliminate Outlaws + Renegade(s)
- [ ] Deputies: protect Sheriff; win with Sheriff
- [ ] Outlaws: eliminate Sheriff
- [ ] Renegade: last player alive
- [ ] Sheriff role is public; all others secret
- [ ] Characters: each has unique ability + starting life points
  - [ ] Sheriff gets +2 life points
- [ ] Life points = bullet tokens; can never exceed starting amount

### Turn

- [ ] Roll 5 dice; re-roll any/all up to 2 times (3 rolls total)
  - [ ] Dice kept after a roll may be re-rolled on the next one
- [ ] Must resolve all dice; can't pass on one
- [ ] Resolve after final roll, in order: Dynamite, Bull's Eyes, Beer, Gatling
- [ ] Arrows resolve immediately after each roll
- [ ] Then pass left

### Dice

- [ ] Arrow: take 1 arrow token each
  - [ ] Take last arrow → Indian attack: each player loses 1 life per arrow they hold, then all arrows are discarded
  - [ ] Roller then takes remaining arrows from that roll
  - [ ] Arrows may be re-rolled
- [ ] Dynamite: can't be re-rolled
  - [ ] 3+ Dynamite → turn ends, lose 1 life
  - [ ] Other dice still resolve
- [ ] Bull's Eye 1: 1 life from adjacent player
- [ ] Bull's Eye 2: 1 life from player 2 places away
  - [ ] Skip eliminated players when counting
  - [ ] 2-3 players left → acts as Bull's Eye 1
- [ ] Beer: any player (incl. self) gains 1 life
  - [ ] Wasted at max life
- [ ] Gatling: 3+ → all other players lose 1 life; discard your arrows

### Elimination & End

- [ ] 0 life → eliminated; reveal role, discard arrows
- [ ] Eliminated players can still win with team
- [ ] Sheriff eliminated: Renegade wins if sole survivor, else Outlaws win
- [ ] All Outlaws + Renegades eliminated: Sheriff + Deputies win
- [ ] Game continues if Outlaws dead but Renegade alive
- [ ] Everyone eliminated at once: Outlaws win
