# Tea Garden

### Overview

- [ ] Theme & vibe
- [ ] Objective: most VPs
- [ ] Game end: 5 rounds

### Round

- [ ] Action phase: take turns clockwise until all pass
  - [ ] Up to 4 turns/round; 4th turn costs 3 Tea Leaves (≥1 brown)
- [ ] Maintenance phase

### Turn

- [ ] Play 1+ cards in leftmost open space below board; total strength = action strength
- [ ] 1 main action + topmost card's secondary action + any free actions, any order
- [ ] Topmost card with 2 secondary symbols: choose 1

### Tea Leaves

- [ ] Basket number = quality (1–6)
- [ ] Green = fresh, loses quality on ripening; brown = fermented, gains quality
- [ ] Green cost: pay green or brown; brown cost: brown only
- [ ] Gained Leaves always green

### Main Actions

- [ ] Build Tea Garden: strength ≥ number below Pagoda on player board
  - [ ] Region adjacent to own garden; River = border
  - [ ] +2 strength to cross 1 extra border (max 1)
  - [ ] Max 1 garden/Region
  - [ ] Region Bonus token: every player who builds there gets it
  - [ ] VP spaces score immediately
- [ ] Buy Action card: strength ≥ number above card; farther left = cheaper
  - [ ] Pay Leaves of minimum quality + have required Teapots
  - [ ] Teapots count from topmost cards played this round only
  - [ ] Missing Teapot: 3 any Leaves or 2 brown Leaves (any quality)
  - [ ] Shift cards left, refill right; new card goes to hand
- [ ] Sell to Caravan: meet 1 trade condition AND 1 strength condition
  - [ ] Trade VPs and bonuses immediate; keep card for Emperor scoring
- [ ] Fermentation: flip strength-many green Leaves to brown; same Basket
- [ ] Extra Leaf Harvest: 1 green Leaf from strength-many gardens; max 1/garden

### Secondary Actions

- [ ] River Navigation: move Boat 1 segment, gain bonus
  - [ ] Segment VPs scored at game end only
  - [ ] 1st to last segment takes highest open VP space
- [ ] Cup Production: take Cup tile from Province where you have a garden
  - [ ] No upside-down tiles; placed tiles never move; new rows OK
  - [ ] Each new connection → Cup token on it
  - [ ] Only same-colour connections score
- [ ] Tea Studies: move Worker 1 sector clockwise, choose 1 half's bonus
  - [ ] Pass start → new Worker in centre (10 VPs)

### Free Actions

- [ ] Need ≥1 card played this turn
- [ ] Emperor track: pay Emperor tokens + 1 Leaf of shown quality → up 1 row, gain reward
  - [ ] No skipping rows
  - [ ] Emperor cards: choose 1 of 3, go to hand, playable this turn
- [ ] Cup token: discard → bonus from 1 adjacent Cup
- [ ] Scrolls: +1 strength, wild Teapot, ×2; one-use; no limit
- [ ] Repeat secondary action: 3 brown Leaves; max 1/turn

### Maintenance Phase

- [ ] Skip in round 5
- [ ] Move Round marker, pass Starting Player token
- [ ] Discard leftmost Action card, shift left, refill
- [ ] Discard played cards
- [ ] Tea Ripening: green moves 1 left, brown 1 right (stop at 1/6)
- [ ] Tea Harvest: 2 green per Tea Garden, quality = Region value
- [ ] Draw 4; unplayed cards stay in hand; no hand limit

### Scoring

- [ ] During game: Region spaces, Caravans, Cup bonuses, other teal VPs
- [ ] Action card VPs
- [ ] Emperor cards: whole deck counts (hand, draw, discard, played)
- [ ] River segment VPs
- [ ] Tea Studies: 10 per centre Worker + rim Worker's sector value
- [ ] Top of Emperor track: 10 VPs
- [ ] Cups: 1/2/4/7/11/16/22 VPs (1–7 same-colour connections)
- [ ] +1 VP/unused Scroll and Emperor token
- [ ] TBs: most Leaves, most brown Leaves
